# Generative-AI use disclosure

OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-5.6 Sol) was used as an interactive computational and writing assistant during development and checking of analysis code, construction of reproducibility workflows, organization of derived outputs, and drafting and revision of manuscript text.

The sole human author, Juan José Pérez Cervera, reviewed the analyses, numerical results, manuscript and reproducibility package; approved the manuscript; and accepts full responsibility for the accuracy, integrity and accountability of the work. No generative-AI image creation was used for scientific figures; figures were produced programmatically from analyzed data. The AI system is not listed as an author.

This disclosure is included in the Methods section because the assistance went beyond AI-assisted copy editing.
